EVIDENCE, NOT THEATER

A hybrid data strategy
with honest boundaries.

Fully synthetic data is useful for novel attacks but weak as proof of realism. Real-only data cannot show attacks that have not happened. FraudGuard combines the strengths without blurring their provenance.
